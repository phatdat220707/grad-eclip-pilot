Interpretation
The protocol can distinguish this simple token baseline from random on the small subset. This weakens an explanation based solely on an uninformative protocol for the prior candidate's poor results, but does not isolate a unique cause or validate the protocol generally. The direct Q/K/V spatial-weight extension underperforms a simpler baseline and should remain a negative candidate result.

Pooling geometry check
For final Transformer output patch tokens t_j and their mean p=(1/N)sum_j t_j, the remaining score has the form s=f(p). Thus ds/dt_j=(1/N) ds/dp for every patch. The output class token is excluded from this mean, so its derivative at that exact output is zero. On all eight images, class-token gradient L1 is exactly zero and maximum inter-patch gradient difference is zero; patch gradients are nonzero. These numerical observations match the pooling structure. This says nothing about class-token influence through earlier attention layers.

The token baseline uses ReLU(sum_c grad(s,t_jc)*t_jc). It is a local intermediate-feature baseline, not an exact pixel attribution or proof of faithfulness. It includes output token activations after the final residual/MLP path, whereas the candidate assigns source-key attention/value contributions. Their difference may matter, but the current experiment does not uniquely prove the reason.
